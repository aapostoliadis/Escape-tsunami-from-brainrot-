🏃 ESCAPE TSUNAMI FOR BRAINROTS! 🌊

Collect Brainrots and bring them to your base before the tsunami hits!

HOW TO PLAY:

  • 🎯 Run down the track to collect Brainrot spheres
  • 🏠 Return to HOME BASE (green) to deposit them for money
  • 🌊 TSUNAMI WAVE comes after 7 seconds - get to safe zones!
  • 🔵 Safe zones (blue platforms) protect you from waves
  • 💰 Earn money to upgrade Speed and Carry Capacity
  • 🔄 Press R to Rebirth for money multipliers
  • 📏 Rarer Brainrots spawn further down the track

CONTROLS:

  • ⌨️ W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys - Move
  • ⌨️ SPACE or ↑ - Jump
  • ⌨️ E - Open Shop
  • ⌨️ R - Rebirth (when available)
